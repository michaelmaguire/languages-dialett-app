# Privacy policy: Dialett da Rögn

The published copy, which Google Play links to, is https://www.michaelmaguire.ca/dialett-da-rogn/privacy/
(michaelmaguire-ca-website); keep the two the same.

*Last updated: 10 November 2026*

Dialett da Rögn (Android package `ca.michaelmaguire.languages.dialett.rogn`) is a dictionary, verb
reference, grammar and phrasebook for the dialect of Arogno, Ticino.

**The app collects no data.** It has no internet access: Android lets an app use the network only if it
asks for that permission, and this app doesn't. Everything it shows is stored inside the app.

- No account or sign-in
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting
- What you type in search stays on your phone: the app never stores or sends it
- Nothing is shared with anyone

The app has two links, which open in your web browser: this policy (published at
https://www.michaelmaguire.ca/dialett-da-rogn/privacy/), and github.com/michaelmaguire/languages-dialett, where the
app's content comes from; what happens on GitHub is covered by GitHub's own privacy statement.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/michaelmaguire/languages-dialett/issues.
